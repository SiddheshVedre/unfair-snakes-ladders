📜 Unfair Rules

Welcome to the least fair board game ever. Here's how to survive:

  • Reach exactly 100 to win. Overshoot and you don't move.
  • Snakes are ruthless: land on a snake's head and slide all the way down.
  • Only three ladders start red: red ladders send you down when you land on their top square; their bottom square is safe. Green ladders take you up from their bottom square.
  • Ladder chaos: every 50 seconds, exactly one randomly selected ladder changes both endpoints by 1–7 squares. It may also change from green/up to red/down or red/down to green/up. There are never more than three red ladders. Ladder effects can chain with snakes and other ladders.
  • 1 to 10 players: choose how many people are playing. Players take turns in order; one player can play solo.
  • 25-second chaos timer: every 25 seconds, only one or two snakes move to nearby positions.
  • Same snake length: when a snake moves, its mouth shifts only 1–7 numbered squares nearby, and its tail follows by the same amount. Its length stays exactly the same.
  • No safe squares for snakes except the ends: a snake's mouth and tail can move anywhere from square 2 to 99, never square 1 or 100.
  • Surprise attack: if a snake's new mouth appears on a player's current square, that snake eats the player and drops them at its tail.
  • Snake bite: landing on a snake's mouth also sends you to its tail.
  • Independent snake movement: when a snake moves, its mouth and tail each shift independently by 1–7 squares. Its numerical length can change.
  • Temporary blinking finish snake: when a player reaches 99, a blinking snake appears on 100 only 3 times out of 10. It vanishes after 50 seconds. Its tail is usually in the 40–60 range (50%), 20–39 (30%), 2–19 (10%), or 61–69 (5%). In the rare 5% golden-snake outcome, its tail reaches 100 and landing on 100 wins.
  • Loop escape: if chained snakes and ladders send you around a loop, you stay on the loop square and your next roll moves normally.
  • 15-second auto-play: if the current player does not roll within 15 seconds, the dice roll automatically and that player's move is played.
  • One roll per turn: no bonus roll for rolling a six.
